Construction in Togo: Buildings and Real Estate Projects

A construction company based in Lomé, Built Assets supports individuals, businesses, institutions and members of the diaspora in delivering their construction projects in Togo.
From planning to handover, we bring our technical expertise to the construction of villas, residential buildings, commercial buildings and institutional facilities, with a constant focus on quality, safety and schedule control.
Our commitment: combining local know-how, transparency and international standards to deliver durable buildings suited to their environment.

Our Expertise: 15 Years of Major Projects and International Standards

For more than 15 years, Built Assets has established itself as a leading player in building construction in Togo and West Africa.

Our experience covers residential, commercial and institutional projects, delivered in partnership with international funders, embassies and local businesses.

Our know-how was forged by our management team's experience on prestigious international projects. We apply rigorous construction standards, tested and proven on complex projects such as the renovation of Barclays Bank in Monaco, and bring the same level of rigour and precision to every site in Togo.

This expertise is carried by a multidisciplinary team of architects, engineers and site managers, able to handle projects from G+1 to G+7, whatever their complexity.

We believe a successful project rests on three pillars: planning, transparency and controlled execution.

The Built Assets Standard: Controlled Execution and On-Time Delivery

Missed deadlines are a common challenge in local construction. We answer it with project management built on rigour and anticipation.

Our working process is designed to eliminate delays and cost overruns, through:

  • Transparent and detailed planning.
  • Optimised coordination of all trades.
  • Clear and regular reporting (essential for the diaspora).

The 3 Phases of Execution: the Built Assets Method

Phase 1: Structural Works and Secure Foundations

We pay particular attention to the structural stability of every building. Our teams master foundation techniques suited to African soils, taking into account the climate and local geotechnical conditions. Every project starts with a precise study to ensure strength, durability and compliance with current standards.

Phase 2: Finishing Works and High-Performance Insulation

Togo's climate calls for a rigorous technical approach. We favour quality local and imported materials, for reliable waterproofing and effective thermal insulation. This phase includes joinery, plumbing, electrical work and painting, carried out under a strict quality-control protocol.

Phase 3: Finishes and Full Compliance

The final stage is about detail. Every element is inspected, tested and approved in line with the specifications, safety standards and local regulations. Our clients receive photographic follow-up, progress reports and a fully documented handover.

FAQ: Construction in Togo

What is the average construction cost per square metre in Togo? The cost varies with the type of building, the finishes and the materials. On average, allow at least FCFA 400,000/m² for a quality residential project, excluding specific interior fit-out.

What are the steps to obtain a building permit in Togo? The application must be submitted by an architect registered with ONATO, together with the plans and technical documents. Built Assets supports you through every administrative step until the permit is granted.

What are the main construction challenges in Lomé? The climate and soil conditions require rigorous execution and reinforced foundations. Our local experience allows us to anticipate these constraints and ensure that buildings last.
