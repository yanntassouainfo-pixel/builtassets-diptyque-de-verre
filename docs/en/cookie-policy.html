Cookie policy

1. What Is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device when you browse a website. It ensures that the website works properly and improves the user experience.

2. Types of Cookies Used

Technical cookies (required)

  • Ensure that the website works
  • Do not require consent

Analytics cookies (with consent)

Example: Google Analytics
Used to understand how the website is used:

  • Pages visited
  • Browsing time
  • Origin of visits

Third-party cookies (if added later)

E.g. videos, maps, social networks
- Require explicit consent

3. Managing Cookies

Users can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Refuse non-essential cookies
  • Customise their choices
  • Withdraw their consent at any time

A “Manage cookies” link must be available in the footer.

4. Retention Period

  • Technical cookies: session length
  • Analytics cookies: 13 months maximum
  • Preference cookies: 6 months
