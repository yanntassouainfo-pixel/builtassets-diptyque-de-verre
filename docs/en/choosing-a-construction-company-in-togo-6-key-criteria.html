Choosing a Construction Company in Togo: 6 Key Criteria

How to Choose the Right Construction Company in Togo: Selection Criteria and Guarantees

Togo’s construction sector continues to expand, driven by major infrastructure projects and growing residential and commercial development, particularly in Lomé. For investors and property owners, choosing the right construction company has therefore become one of the most important decisions in any building project.

With many contractors and service providers operating in the market, how can you distinguish a professional construction company from an informal contractor?

Choosing the wrong partner can turn an ambitious project into a financial burden, with construction defects, uncontrolled costs and repeated delays. This guide outlines the key criteria to consider when selecting a construction company in Togo.

1. Technical Expertise and References: The Foundation of Trust

A reliable construction company should first be assessed on its track record and ability to manage technical complexity.

In Togo, varying soil conditions, low-lying areas and coastal environments can create specific engineering challenges that require strong civil engineering expertise.

  • Portfolio: Do not rely on promises alone. A professional company should be able to present references from completed or ongoing projects, including multi-storey buildings, commercial developments, offices or institutional projects.
  • International standards: Experience working on demanding projects for international institutions, embassies, banks or major organisations can demonstrate the ability to operate within rigorous technical and quality-control frameworks.

At Built Assets, we apply structured construction methods and quality standards developed through experience on demanding projects.

2. Building Construction vs Public Works: What Expertise Does Your Project Require?

Although they are often grouped together under the construction sector, building construction and public works require different areas of expertise.

Building construction focuses primarily on residential, commercial and institutional buildings, while public works generally involve infrastructure such as roads, drainage networks and urban development.

For a building project in Lomé, these disciplines can overlap. Expertise in infrastructure and drainage may be important for connecting the property to its surroundings, while building expertise determines structural quality, finishes and thermal comfort.

Choosing a company with the right combination of skills is therefore essential.

3. Design-Build: An Integrated Approach to Construction in Togo

One of the recurring risks in construction projects is fragmentation between the architect, structural engineer and contractor.

When responsibilities are poorly coordinated, misunderstandings between design and execution can result in delays, technical inconsistencies and unexpected costs.

An integrated design-build approach provides a single point of coordination throughout the project.

At Built Assets, this model can provide several advantages:

  • More efficient project delivery: Design and construction planning can be coordinated from the beginning.
  • Better budget control: Construction costs can be considered during the technical design phase rather than after the project has already been designed.
  • Consistent quality: Technical decisions can be coordinated between the design team and the professionals responsible for execution on site.

4. Legal Guarantees and Administrative Support

Working with a formally established construction company provides greater contractual and administrative protection.

Before appointing a contractor in Togo, several elements should be verified:

  • RCCM registration and tax compliance: These documents help confirm the legal existence and formal status of the company.
  • Building permit: The company should be able to assist with the technical documentation required for obtaining a building permit in Togo.
  • Insurance: Request evidence of the professional insurance coverage applicable to the company and your specific project.

These documents should be checked before signing the construction contract.

5. Checklist: 5 Documents to Request Before Signing

Before appointing a construction company, request clear documentation covering the scope, cost, schedule and responsibilities of the project.

  • Detailed Bill of Quantities / Cost Estimate: Avoid vague lump-sum quotations. Quantities, specifications and unit prices should be clearly identified.
  • Gantt Schedule: A detailed construction programme showing the different stages and expected completion dates.
  • Insurance Certificates: Check that the relevant policies are valid and appropriate for the project.
  • Health and Safety Plan: The project should include appropriate measures for worker safety and site risk management.
  • Construction Contract: The contract should clearly define the scope of work, responsibilities, deadlines, payment terms and provisions relating to delays or disputes.

6. Sustainable Innovation: Creating Long-Term Value in 2026

A modern construction company should do more than simply build walls. It should consider the long-term performance of the property.

Energy efficiency, water management, passive cooling and appropriate material selection can reduce operating costs while improving comfort.

When selecting a construction partner, consider whether the company can integrate sustainable building solutions and recognised frameworks such as EDGE certification in Togo.

Planning these measures from the design stage can improve the environmental performance and long-term attractiveness of the property.

FAQ: Choosing a Construction Partner in Togo

Is It Risky to Choose the Cheapest Construction Company?

The lowest quotation is not necessarily the lowest final cost.

An unusually low price may result from differences in material specifications, technical scope, supervision, insurance or other services included in the quotation.

Rather than comparing the final price alone, compare exactly what each contractor has included and excluded.

How Does Built Assets Provide Construction Monitoring for Diaspora Clients?

How Long Does It Take to Start Construction After Signing?

The mobilisation period depends on the project, administrative requirements, technical studies, procurement and site conditions.

Once the required approvals and technical studies have been completed, the construction company can establish a mobilisation schedule and confirm the planned start date.

Conclusion: Invest in Peace of Mind

Choosing your construction company is one of the foundations of a successful property project.

By prioritising technical expertise, transparency, documentation and rigorous project management, you can significantly reduce the risks associated with construction.
