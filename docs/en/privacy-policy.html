Privacy policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Built Assets collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of users of its website, in accordance with:

  • Togolese Law No. 2019-014 on the protection of personal data,
  • international good practice (inspired by the GDPR).

By using this website, users accept these terms.

2. Data Controller

Data controller: The Management
Email: info@builtassets.com
Phone: +228 22 22 15 63 / 91 08 09 09

3. Data Collected

3.1 Data provided voluntarily through the contact form

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Content of the message
  • Information about a project or need (if provided voluntarily)

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • IP address
  • Device type, browser, operating system
  • Cookies (see the Cookie Policy below)
  • Anonymous statistical data (Google Analytics or equivalent)

4. Purposes of Processing

Data is collected in order to:

  • Respond to contact requests
  • Provide quotations or commercial information
  • Follow up with clients and prospects
  • Manage and improve our services
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the website

Produce anonymous visitor statistics

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent (when the contact form is submitted)
  • Legitimate interest of Built Assets (commercial management, internal communication)
  • Pre-contractual steps (e.g. project study, quotation)

6. Retention Period

  • 12 months: requests with no commercial follow-up
  • 5 years: client files, projects, professional correspondence
  • Legal periods: contractual documents, accounting records

7. Recipients of the Data

Data is accessible only to:

  • The Management,
  • authorised Built Assets staff,
  • technical providers involved in hosting or maintaining the website (Hostinger), who are bound by confidentiality.

No data is sold or transferred to unauthorised third parties.

8. Transfers of Data Outside Togo

Data may be hosted within the European Union or in countries offering an adequate level of protection. Built Assets ensures that these transfers comply with legal obligations.

9. Users' Rights

Users have the following rights:

  • Access
  • Rectification
  • Erasure
  • Restriction of processing
  • Objection
  • Withdrawal of consent

To exercise these rights:
📧 info@builtassets.com

Users may also contact the competent authority:

APDP, Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (Togo's personal data protection authority).

10. Security

Built Assets applies the following security measures:

  • Secure hosting
  • HTTPS encryption
  • Restricted access to internal data
  • Protection against abusive form submissions
  • Technical backups

11. Cookies

Cookies are described in detail in the Cookie Policy.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated.
