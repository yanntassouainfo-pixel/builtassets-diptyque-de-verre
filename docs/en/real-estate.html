Real Estate and Land Asset Development with Built Assets

At Built Assets, we understand that maximizing the value of your real estate and land assets is key to the success of your projects.
That’s why we support you at every stage of the process, providing tailored advice and customized solutions to help you enhance and optimize the value of your property portfolio.

We conduct an in-depth analysis of your assets, taking into account your financial and strategic objectives to determine the best development opportunities.
Our services include:

  • Real estate market studies: We analyze local and regional market trends to provide an accurate and optimal valuation of your property.
  • Real estate development: We manage end-to-end development projects, from design and construction to marketing and sales.
  • Asset management: We help you manage your property portfolio, optimizing profitability while minimizing risk.

Member of ANAIT and FTI

As an active member of ANAIT (National Association of Real Estate Agents of Togo) and FTI (Togolese Real Estate Federation), Built Assets is committed to upholding the ethical and professional standards of the real estate industry in Togo.
This membership ensures that our clients benefit from a trusted professional network and compliance with industry best practices for every real estate project.

Why Choose Built Assets for Your Real Estate Projects?

As a real estate expert in West Africa, Built Assets combines technical expertise and in-depth local knowledge to help you:

  • Maximize the value of your land and property assets.
  • Optimize the profitability of your real estate projects.
  • Manage your investments efficiently with the support of our certified real estate agents and partner architects.

Contact us to discuss your real estate project and explore how we can help you bring it to life.
