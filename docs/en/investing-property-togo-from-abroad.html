Investing in Property in Togo from Abroad: Your Project, Our Expertise, Zero Stress

Distance should not stand in the way of your ambitions.

Whether you want to build your dream home, invest in rental property or create a long-term asset for your family, Built Assets can act as your trusted partner on the ground in Togo.

Our role is to turn the complexity of managing a property project remotely into a structured, transparent and professionally supervised process.

A Project That Adapts to Your Budget: Build at Your Own Pace

One of the biggest concerns when investing from abroad is financing.

Do you need the entire construction budget before starting? Not necessarily.

Through our Owner’s Representation / Assistance à Maîtrise d’Ouvrage (AMO) approach, your project can be structured around clearly defined phases:

  • Phased construction: We divide the project into logical stages (foundations, structural works and finishes) allowing you to finance the project progressively.
  • Cash-flow flexibility: If you need to pause construction while rebuilding your available funds, the project can be secured before work resumes.
  • Better budget visibility: Each phase is assessed and budgeted in advance to reduce the risk of unexpected additional costs.

Greater Peace of Mind: Secure Your Land Before You Build

Land acquisition in Togo requires careful verification. Before construction begins, the legal and administrative status of the property should be properly assessed.

Built Assets can assist with the process through:

  • Land documentation review: Examination of available cadastral and ownership documentation to help identify potential inconsistencies or risks.
  • Land title procedures: Coordination and support with the relevant professionals and authorities involved in formalising property rights.
  • On-the-ground verification: Physical site verification and coordination of boundary-related procedures where required.

The objective is simple: clarify the status of the land before committing significant construction funds.

Your Construction Project in Your Pocket: Remote Monitoring

Built Assets helps reduce the challenges of distance through structured and transparent reporting.

  • Visual progress updates: Regular photographs and videos documenting construction progress, materials and workmanship.
  • Technical reports: Written updates covering progress against the construction schedule, technical observations and budget monitoring.
  • Direct communication: A dedicated communication channel and regular online meetings can keep you connected to the project whether you are based in Europe, North America or elsewhere.

You remain informed about your project without having to be permanently present in Togo.
