Design & Build

The one-stop solution for faster delivery and single-point accountability.

Design & Build is an innovative and integrated project delivery system that has rapidly become the preferred choice for many clients.
This model combines both design management and construction execution into a single, streamlined process, saving time and money while fostering close collaboration among all stakeholders.

By choosing the Design & Build approach, you work with a single point of contact, ensuring that your entire project is managed efficiently and transparently.
This centralized model simplifies coordination between architects, engineers, designers, and construction professionals, resulting in greater consistency and performance throughout the process.

Our team of architectural design and construction experts works closely with you to bring your shared vision to life.
We handle every stage (from planning and detailed design to construction) to ensure a seamless experience and optimal results.

The advantages of our Design & Build services include:

  • Enhanced collaboration between teams, ensuring consistency and efficiency.
  • Improved project performance through an integrated, end-to-end approach.
  • Clearer communication, reducing risks of errors or misunderstandings.
  • Time savings through optimized planning and execution.
  • Cost reduction by eliminating redundancies and optimizing resources.
  • Increased accountability with centralized project management.
  • Comprehensive expertise covering both design and construction.

With Built Assets, you can rely on our expertise to deliver your construction project quickly, efficiently, and to the highest quality standards, while meeting your objectives with full transparency and precision.
