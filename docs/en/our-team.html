The Built Assets team
01Where we come from

Experience gained in the West, adapted to Africa.

After gaining solid experience in the construction industry in the West, we recognized the importance of adapting methods and solutions to each context, particularly in Africa. This led us to establish a construction company that offers an innovative approach tailored to the realities of Africa.

Our goal is to contribute to the development of sustainable and resilient living environments in Africa by providing effective, accessible, and environmentally friendly construction solutions.
