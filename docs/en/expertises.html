Construction, AMO and real estate in West Africa

Built Assets is your single partner for securing and delivering construction and real estate projects in Togo and West Africa.

With our integrated approach, we combine the rigour of project management assistance (AMO), the creativity of architectural design and excellence in the execution of works (construction). Whether you are a local investor, a member of the diaspora or an institution, our teams guide you towards performance, sustainability (EDGE) and the success of your assets.
