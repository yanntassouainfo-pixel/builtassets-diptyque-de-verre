What is Project Management Assistance (AMO)?

Project Management Assistance (AMO) provides strategic and technical support to project owners in the successful delivery of their construction or real estate projects.
Our role is to represent your interests, ensuring that every stage (from design to completion) meets your goals, budget, and quality standards.

At Built Assets, our AMO service in Togo and West Africa guides you throughout the entire process, whether you are building, developing, or renovating.
We combine local expertise with international best practices to guarantee transparent, efficient, and secure project management.

Our services include:

  • Defining project requirements aligned with your objectives.
  • Selecting reliable and qualified partners.
  • Managing timelines, budgets, and compliance standards.
  • Overseeing construction to ensure quality and safety at every step.

Our personalized approach ensures that your project moves forward with confidence, from the first idea to the finished structure.
